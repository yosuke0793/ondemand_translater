TranslateService distribution notices
====================================

TranslateService GUI/source components are MIT licensed; see
TranslateService-LICENSE.txt. TranslateWorker bundles Piper and its
GPL-licensed eSpeak-NG bridge; the worker and complete distribution therefore
include components not covered by the application's MIT license.
The separately executed FFmpeg/ffprobe programs are NOT covered by that license.

FFmpeg / ffprobe
---------------
FFmpeg and ffprobe are built from hash-pinned upstream source archives,
with GPLv3 enabled and nonfree components forbidden. They are separately
executed programs; the application's MIT license does not replace their GPL.
Sources, versions, license texts and the build recipe are recorded in
ffmpeg/source-manifest.json and the accompanying corresponding-source archive.
Upstream references:
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

Each release includes TranslateService-<version>-<target>-ffmpeg-source.tar.gz
next to its platform installer/DMG. Keep that archive available with the
binaries; it contains the exact original source archives, recipe and build
records for FFmpeg and its enabled external libraries. See the bundled
ffmpeg notices for copyright/license texts. A URL to latest upstream sources
alone is not a substitute. Do not describe the complete bundle as MIT-only.
FreeType is used under the FreeType License (FTL); its complete license is
included under ffmpeg/freetype-2.13.3.tar.xz/docs/FTL.TXT.
Portions of this software are copyright (C) 2024 The FreeType Project
(www.freetype.org). All rights reserved.
The former eugeneware/ffmpeg-static Apple Silicon binary reports that it
contains nonfree parts and is not legally redistributable; do not distribute
old artifacts built with that binary.
Draft releases are for review; do not publish until licensing obligations
and the security/signing checklist in docs/PACKAGING.md have been reviewed.

PyAV and its managed FFmpeg shared libraries
--------------------------------------------
PyAV 15.1.0 is built from its hash-pinned PyPI source and is licensed under
BSD-3-Clause. Its shared FFmpeg 7.1.1 libraries are built from the pinned
FFmpeg source with a decoding-only, shared LGPL-2.1-or-later configuration.
GPL, version-3-only, nonfree, x264/x265, encoders, muxers and network protocols
are disabled for this separate PyAV build. The exact license texts are under
pyav/. Corresponding sources, recipe, build records and license texts are
published as TranslateService-<version>-<target>-pyav-source.tar.gz. Keep this
archive with the package; it is separate from the GPL FFmpeg executable source
archive above.

Python dependencies
-------------------
python-distributions.json identifies installed distribution versions/license
metadata. Wheel-provided license/notice texts are retained under python/.
dependency-lock.txt records the hash-locked native build dependency graph.
Some upstream wheels do not include full license texts; review their
metadata and obtain any missing required notices before public publication.
Version-matched supplemental texts for CTranslate2 4.6.0, FlatBuffers
25.12.19, SentencePiece 0.2.0 and Tokenizers 0.22.1 are included under
python/<distribution>/. Their upstream URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded
in supplemental-license-manifest.json. CTranslate2's seven pinned source
submodule licenses and SentencePiece's four bundled dependency licenses are
also retained conservatively; inclusion does not assert that every component
is present in every platform wheel. These supplements are not a claim that
all native-library or Rust dependency notices have been exhaustively reviewed.

Piper text-to-speech
--------------------
The offline Piper runtime (piper-tts 1.3.0) is licensed under
GPL-3.0-or-later. Piper's espeakbridge extension statically links eSpeak-NG,
also GPL-3.0-or-later. The required corresponding source, including exact
hash-pinned Piper/eSpeak-NG source archives, the source manifest, and the
allowlisted application/build source with SHA-256 inventory, is
distributed beside each platform artifact as
TranslateService-<version>-<target>-piper-source.tar.gz. Do not distribute a
Piper-enabled binary without its matching source archive and the required GPL
license texts. Piper and eSpeak-NG license texts from their exact source
revisions are included under third-party-notices/piper/. Piper package notices
and license files are retained under third-party-notices/python/ when supplied
by their wheels.

Piper is imported into and linked with the frozen TranslateWorker. Accordingly,
the Piper-enabled combined worker distribution is provided under
GPL-3.0-or-later. The root MIT license continues to apply to separately
applicable components; it is not a blanket MIT-only license for this combined
distribution. The root LICENSE and the complete allowlisted application source
are included in the corresponding-source archive.

The bundled offline voice is en_US-ljspeech-medium, trained by Bryce Beattie
using the LJ Speech Dataset. The creator identifies the model as public domain;
its model card also identifies the underlying recordings as public domain.
The exact model, configuration and model card are hash-pinned in
piper_model.json and included in the offline application bundle together with
the license/provenance evidence. The pinned Hugging Face model files are not
claimed to be byte-identical to the creator page's separate mv2 model downloads.
Provenance:
https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/c10ece1aade47bb51c153c893d14e5bf8e5b7117/en/en_US/ljspeech/medium

Argos language packs and optional Tesseract/Ollama installations are not
included and have separate licenses.
