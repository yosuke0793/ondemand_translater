Piper John voice license and provenance evidence
===============================================

Bundled identifier: en_US-john-medium
Creator: Bryce Beattie
Creator page: https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/

The creator page's John row describes a US English male single-speaker voice,
finetuned from Kristin on medium quality settings for an additional 600 epochs.
It states that the dataset contains about 12.5 hours of recordings, all from
LibriVox.org, and labels this voice "License: public domain".

The John row links these exact original model and configuration downloads:
https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/john.onnx
https://sfo3.digitaloceanspaces.com/bkmdls/john.onnx.json

The original downloads are renamed locally to en_US-john-medium.onnx and
en_US-john-medium.onnx.json without modifying their bytes. These HTTPS URLs
are mutable; piper_model.json pins the SHA-256 of each observed original.
Downloads and caches must match those hashes; no unpinned replacement is used.
The original configuration declares one speaker, a 22050 Hz sample rate,
espeak phonemes, and piper_version 1.0.0. Runtime compatibility is verified
with the pinned piper-tts 1.3.0 engine rather than inferred from that field.

Creator licensing evidence and original assets consulted 2026-10-10.
